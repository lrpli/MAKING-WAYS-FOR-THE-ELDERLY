About Our Work

Building Reliable Support for Older Adults

Making Ways for the Elderly is dedicated to helping senior community members access support systems, maintain dignity, and remain connected to the people and places they value most.

EIN: 99-5096901

Core Focus

  • Dignity Protecting the worth and autonomy of every senior.
  • Access Connecting elderly individuals to practical resources.
  • Community Strengthening relationships across generations.
Our mission is to improve quality of life for elderly individuals through compassionate service, responsive support, and meaningful community engagement in Macon, Georgia.

Our Values

Everything we do is guided by three foundational principles that shape our service to the community.

Compassion

We lead with empathy and respect in every interaction, ensuring every senior feels valued and heard.

Consistency

We show up reliably for the people and families we serve, building trust through dependable action.

Integrity

We honor transparency and accountability in our work, maintaining the highest ethical standards.

Leadership

Our organization is led by dedicated individuals committed to serving the elderly community.
